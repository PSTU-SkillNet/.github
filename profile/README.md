### 🌟 About SkillNet

**SkillNet** is a dynamic university-to-industry networking initiative dedicated to bridging the gap between academic learning and professional success. Originating from **Patuakhali Science and Technology University (PSTU)** and expanding across the Barishal Division, our ultimate vision is to establish a nationwide collaborative ecosystem across Bangladesh.

We bring together **students, universities, alumni, industry leaders, and multinational employers** to build meaningful career pathways, provide real-world industry exposure, and cultivate future-ready talent.

> *"We believe that every student deserves access to the right connections, meaningful opportunities, and the mentorship needed to transform their academic journey into a flourishing professional career."*

### 💻 Open Source & Technology at PSTU-SkillNet

As the GitHub organization for SkillNet, we foster open-source development, collaborative software projects, and digital tools built by and for university students:

- 🛠️ **Campus Platforms**: Community-driven portals, event management systems, and alumni databases.
- 📚 **Learning Roadmaps**: Curated learning repositories for programming, cloud, machine learning, and interview preparation.
- 💡 **Inter-disciplinary Innovation**: Bringing together students from Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), EEE, ICT, Agriculture, Fisheries, BBA, and beyond to build impactful projects.

> 🤝 **Want to contribute?** Fork any of our public repositories, submit pull requests, or reach out to start a new collaborative project under the `PSTU-SkillNet` umbrella!
